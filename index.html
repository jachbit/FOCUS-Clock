Getting startedClick or tap the page once: browsers only let a page speak after that. The 🔊 / 🔇 icon under the clock (also in compact mode) turns the voice on or off; the first time it starts at every 30 minutes. Interval, voice and speed: ⚙ Settings (▶ Test there).
Compact mode▭ Compact or H hides the header and sizes the clock to the window: ideal for a small window in a corner of a big screen. Move the mouse over it for ☰ Header (or press H again) to bring the header and Settings back.
KeysH compact on / off  ·  F full screen on / off  ·  V voice on / off  ·  T swap the main clock  ·  Esc closes this panel and Settings (and leaves full screen). In full screen the ⛶ Full button turns into ↧ Min.
Corner windowIn Microsoft Edge: ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app. It opens in its own window with no address bar; drag it to a corner and size it.
Talking clock"It's 10:30 AM Eastern": every 15, 30 or 60 minutes, all day or during market hours only, in the voice and speed you pick (the voices installed on this device; Edge has the most natural ones).
Local timeOff by default. Tick ⚙ Settings → "Also say my local time" and it adds this device's own time zone after Eastern: "It's 10:30 AM Eastern, 7:30 AM Pacific" (Central, Mountain, London... whatever the device is set to). On a device already on Eastern time it says Eastern only, so it never repeats itself.
Market sessionNYSE hours in Eastern Time: pre-market 4:00, open 9:30, close 16:00, after hours until 20:00, with the countdown to the next change. Holidays and 1:00 PM early closes for 2026-2027 are built in.
Main clockEastern (New York) by default. In ⚙ Settings → Main clock pick "My local time" (this device's zone, e.g. Pacific) or a city: the big clock and the date follow it, New York moves into the world clocks (first, not counted in the 4), and the talking clock leads with your zone ("It's 7:30 AM Pacific"; tick "Also say Eastern time" for "..., 10:30 AM Eastern"). The market session, the countdown and "market hours only" always stay on New York time, and so does the small header clock. Shortcut: the ⇄ button under the clock (also in compact mode) or T swaps the main clock between New York and your other zone (⇄ PT / ⇄ ET); the other zone is your last non-Eastern choice in Settings, else your local time.
World clocksUp to 4 cities in ⚙ Settings (plus New York when the main clock is not Eastern); +1 / -1 when their date differs from the main clock.
PrivacyNo account and no tracking. Your settings stay in this browser, so each device keeps its own.
